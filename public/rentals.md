## How you keep them

**Every stay turns into a guest you can reach.**

1. During the stay: They use your app for codes, picks and questions.
2. After checkout: They get your updates by push, email or text.
3. Next trip: They book you direct, and you keep the whole payment.

Airbnb keeps about 15.5% of every booking. A direct booking keeps it with you.

## Meet Shorty, your house's front desk

He answers. He guides. He keeps in touch.

### Answers, trained by you

**Every answer, any hour. In your words.**

Tell Shorty about your house once, the way you'd tell a friend. Wi-Fi, door code, checkout, the pool, the TV. He answers guests from what you taught him and never makes things up.

Guests can still reach you. Shorty gives them your preferred way to get in touch, and lets you know when something needs you.

Example house notes: "Pool closes at 9. No glass out there." "Extra towels: hall closet, top shelf." "Checkout 10am. Trash to the curb." "Something broken? Text me." (The house, the host and the phone number shown on the page are made-up demo content.)

### The best of the area, from you

**The things that make a trip memorable.**

Add your top local restaurants, rainy day activities, beach spots, kids' favorites and local perks, with your own notes. When a guest has the best taco of their trip, it came from your app.

### Keeps in touch

**Texts, emails and push notifications. From your app.**

A welcome before they arrive. A heads-up when it rains. A checkout reminder. Then, months later, a note that their week is open again. You stay in their pocket, not the booking site.

## The stay they talk about

**A house with its own app feels like a better house.**

The group shares it before they land. They find the taco truck you love, the rainy day plan, the discount down the street. The trip goes better, and your place is the reason.

## New guests, too

**People ask AI where to stay now. We point them to you.**

We're built into ChatGPT. When a traveler asks for a rental in your area, your place can come up. They open your app, ask Shorty anything, and request dates with you directly. Your address stays private until you're ready to share it.

## Getting them in

**Send your app however you already reach guests.**

- Text: Before check-in, from your phone.
- Email: In your welcome email.
- Link: Anywhere you already talk.
- QR code: A card in the house for the group.

## Runs your socials, too

**Connect your Instagram and Facebook. Shorty handles the rest.**

He posts for you, answers comments and replies to DMs, all from what you've taught him. Someone asks "is it pet friendly?" at midnight, and they get an answer and a link to your app.

## Book direct

**We build the cart. Stripe and Square move the money.**

A guest requests dates, you confirm, they pay through your own Stripe or Square. It lands in your bank. No booking-site fee, and we never touch it.

- Booking fee on a direct booking: $0
- Where the money goes: Your bank
- Whose guests they are: Yours
